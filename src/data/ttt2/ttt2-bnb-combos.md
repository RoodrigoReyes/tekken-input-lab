Solo Combos, Tag Assaults Fillers & Enders

Solo Combo routes, Tag Assault fillers and enders for open ground and the wall, and execution notes, organized by character compiled from Tekken Zaibatsu, Youtube, and some of my nerdy friends brains, Routes, alternatives, and corrections have not been independently tested, but will be updated accordingly (or when you tell me to).

The idea is, you learn your character’s initial combo theory, and you should be able to mix & match them with the respective TA fillers and enders available. This game has over 1700 possible team combinations, I’m sorry I don’t have the optimal combo for your Zafina/Baek team, but the beauty of this game is using your creativity to find tech for your own team if it isn’t already out there.
