Big Ol' Disclaimer

8
Aight so look, this was initially made for myself and my local guys so I didn’t have to keep opening up Tekken Zaibatsu on WayBack and waiting an eternity for a page to load. It's not gonna be flawless, but there's a fair amout of work put in.

The combos inside are picked by me based on a few things but primarily Tournament Viability, picked from a combination of TZ threads, memory, and watching a shit tonne of old footage. I've kept the list to tournamant BnBs and some max routes only, but there are more routes you can find on TZ.

The combo's I picked probably aint max damage but if it's good enough to use in Tekken Crash its good enough for you.

There will be a few small additions such as a character specific glossary and list of good players for each character. The Players I've listed are either picked from memory or because I saw them do some hard ass shit in a tournament and assumed they were a specialist (back when that used to matter). It is very Korean focused as I am an unapolagetic glazer of the Korean scene.

Last thing, some characters are just gonna be more fleshed out than others. I'm only one dude I'm not gonna be able to find every guaranteed hit or niche off axis combo yano?
